Vlog Intro Collection, 2023

Motion Graphics / Animated Title Sequences

A collection of animated vlog intro designs exploring typography, rhythm, and visual identity through motion.

Role

Motion Design / Typography / Visual Direction / Editing

Medium

Animated Title Sequence / Motion Graphics / Video Compilation

Tools

After Effects / Processing / Video Editing

Overview

This video is a compilation of vlog intro designs and animated title sequences, created as a personal exploration of motion graphics. Each piece experiments with the relationship between form, typography, and rhythm across different imagined vlog styles, from minimal aesthetics to hyperpop-inspired visuals.
