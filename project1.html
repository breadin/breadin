Divergital, 2025

3D / AI Video Art Direction

Divergital is a 3D and AI-generated video project exploring individuality within digital systems.

Role

Concept / 3D Visual Development / AI Video Direction / Art Direction

Medium

3D Visuals / AI Video Generation / Large-scale Projection

Tools

Blender / Stable Diffusion / Video Editing

Concept

As technology advances, it can amplify biases and reinforce uniform ways of seeing the world. Divergital imagines a rigid digital landscape where a lone organic horse appears as a symbol of difference within a system built on uniformity.

Rather than losing its essence, the horse integrates with technology and evolves into a cybernetic unicorn — a new form that bridges the organic and the technological.

Transformation Narrative

The project follows a transformation from difference to integration and divergence. The horse enters a digital system, merges with mechanical structures, and evolves into a cybernetic unicorn — a symbol of difference, transformation, and interconnected progress.

Video

The project uses 3D modeling in Blender as a structural foundation, combined with Stable Diffusion to generate a distinct visual texture. It explores the relationship between precise 3D control and the latent possibilities of generative AI.

Exhibition View

Divergital was presented as a large-scale video installation, extending the digital image into a spatial viewing experience.
